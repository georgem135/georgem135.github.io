A little about me

Practical experience. Curious mindset.

I'm building my way into data analytics through hands-on projects and steady technical learning.

Why analytics?

Before focusing on analytics, I worked in administration and healthcare support. My work included patient bookings, record keeping, data capture, spreadsheet tracking and file checks. It taught me how important accuracy, confidentiality and organised information are.

I'm now building on that experience with data tools. My portfolio is a place to practise, learn from mistakes, and get better at turning a business question into a report someone can understand.

Outside of analytics, I enjoy video games, reading and spending time with friends.

What I'm working towards

I want to grow into a dependable analyst who can prepare data carefully, build useful reports, explain findings clearly and keep learning from feedback.

  • Recommendations supported by evidence
